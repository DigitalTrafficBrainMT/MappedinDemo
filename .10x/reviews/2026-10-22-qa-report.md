## Known issues (not blocking)
1. **Duplicate room names**, such as two "Electrical Room · Level 2", look identical in lists. This is a data issue.
2. **Overview labels.** At the whole-floor overview, the SDK's collision rules hide most room labels; they appear on zoom-in.
3. **Reduced motion** wasn't emulated. It was verified by code review: every animation passes through `motion()`, and CSS respects the `prefers-reduced-motion` media query.
4. **1440×900 screenshots.** In this test browser, captures at 1440×900 came out at quarter scale after a reload. The layout itself was verified at 1440×900 through the DOM (`innerWidth` 1440); visual sign-off used 1280×800.
5. **No e2e automation.** UI checks are manual. If the demo keeps evolving, a Playwright smoke test of this journey is the recommended next step.
